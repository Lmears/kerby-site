KerBy

Kick and bass in one instrument Hold a note and KerBy plays a groove, or write every hit yourself. Draw the sounds, bring in samples, and decide how the kick and bass share the low end. Two ways to play Live: hold a note and each voice pulses at its own rate, in time with your DAW. Ride Rate for stutters and half-time, then hit home to snap back to the groove. The bass step row mutes and re-pitches individual steps, so one held note becomes a bassline. Draw: write the rhythm in your piano roll, one hit per note. Make the sound Draw the waveform, then draw how each hit moves in volume, pitch and filter. Or drop an audio file onto either voice. Click adds a sampled attack on top of the kick. From there: FM, saturation, noise, downsampling, and a reverb on the kick for low rumble. Glitch adds rolls and stutters that stay in time. Kick and bass together Auto-align: never fix phase again. It lines your bass up with the kick on every hit, so the low end stays full even when the note changes. On by default. Ducking is on from the start. Draw your own curve, let the two rhythms drift apart, or turn it off. The modulation matrix routes macros, LFOs and envelopes across both voices, so one knob can move the whole groove. Resample Drag from a scope and that bar lands in your DAW as audio. Drop it back on a Wave tab and KerBy plays its own output. What you get macOS 11 or later: AU and VST3.
